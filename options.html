Analysis Engine

⚠️
Privacy Notice: Enabling LLM mode sends your message draft text directly to your specified AI provider. Your API key and drafts are stored strictly on your device (chrome.storage.local) and are never routed through any intermediary servers.

What Data Does This Use?

Zero telemetry or analytics: Emotion Context does not bundle any analytics libraries, telemetry probes, or tracking scripts.

Zero external servers: This open-source project operates no backends or proxy servers whatsoever.

Ephemeral in-memory analysis: In local mode, all heuristic evaluations happen synchronously in memory. Drafts and detected tones are discarded immediately.

Strict minimum permissions: We request only host permissions for ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini plus local storage for configuration.
